Pump tracks for Leeston

Built for local riders.

Smaller towns like Leeston often suit a single community loop that schools, families and visiting riders can share. We help community groups with concept images and cost breakdowns for funding applications.

Local site considerations

Low-lying ground in parts of the Ellesmere area makes drainage design and finished levels a priority from day one.

Working with Selwyn District Council

For council-owned reserves we can prepare concept designs, drawings and cost breakdowns to support conversations with Selwyn District Council parks and community teams. For schools, we work directly with your principal and board. Check consent requirements with the council at selwyn.govt.nz.

Build types

Track only surfaces just the riding line (typically 1.5 m wide); Fill in surfaces the whole area. Both are priced at $400–$600 per surfaced m², excl. GST.
